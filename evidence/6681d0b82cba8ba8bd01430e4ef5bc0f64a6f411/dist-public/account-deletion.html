Delete a Dukan ERP account

These instructions concern Dukan ERP accounts. Dukan ERP is not yet open, so no Dukan ERP account exists yet, and browsing this website does not create one.

What this page covers

This page is about accounts in Dukan ERP, the product operated under the TSX-MR name. Browsing the TSX-MR public website does not create an account, so visiting it leaves nothing to delete.

Before Dukan ERP opens

Dukan ERP is not yet open for public registration, so no Dukan ERP account exists yet. There is nothing to delete today.

When Dukan ERP opens

Accounts will be deleted from inside the Dukan ERP mobile app. Before the service opens, this page will explain each step: how identity is confirmed, what is deleted and what has to be kept.

Information sent by email or another enquiry

Deleting a Dukan ERP account and deleting personal information you sent us are separate requests. If you have given TSX-MR personal information in another way, for example by email, by telephone or through WhatsApp, write to privacy@tsxmr.com to ask for it to be deleted. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We then delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it, and tell you when it is done.
