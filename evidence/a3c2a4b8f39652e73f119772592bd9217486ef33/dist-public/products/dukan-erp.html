What Dukan ERP replaces

The daily tools that cost shops time and money.

  • Notebooks

    Stock, credit and cash on paper are slow to check and easy to lose.

  • Scattered messages

    Orders, prices and reminders spread across chats that nobody can search later.

  • Uncertain stock

    Nobody knows for sure what is on the shelf, what was sold and what came back.

  • Unclear money positions

    Cash, mobile accounts and customer debts that never quite add up at closing time.

  • Uncontrolled access

    Employees who can see or change anything, with no record of who did what.

Two activities, one platform

Dukan ERP is being built for two business activities. Each store of a business will run one of them or both.

Retail & Electronics

For shops that sell phones, electronics, accessories and other goods.

  • Inventory and product models, with specifications, quantities and prices
  • IMEI and serial-number tracking for every serialised device
  • Receiving stock by scanning or typing, and importing supplier files
  • Sales, with the receipt
  • Payment channels: cash and the payment accounts the business configures
  • Cash and account positions, counted and closed every day
  • Returns and refunds
  • Expenses
  • Daily, monthly and yearly reporting
  • Staff and store permissions

Money Services Agent

For agents who exchange cash for mobile-money credit on behalf of their customers.

  • Recording exchanges in both directions: cash received for provider credit sent, and provider credit received for cash paid out
  • The commission is paid by the customer and typed by the employee for every exchange — there is no fixed rate
  • The commission is the business’s own revenue: the provider neither pays nor checks it, and it never changes the provider credit
  • Before confirming, the employee sees the principal, the commission, the cash received or handed over, and the credit sent or received
  • The employee who recorded each exchange is taken from their own sign-in, never chosen by hand
  • Activity, floats and commissions followed provider by provider
  • History and reports by day, week, month and year
  • Customer numbers masked in lists and visible in full only with the permission

A business will be able to use either activity or both, store by store.

About mobile-money providers

Dukan ERP records the exchanges that the agent’s team enters. It is not connected to any mobile-money provider and does not check a provider’s balance. No provider has approved, integrated with or endorsed Dukan ERP.

Trust, privacy and support

These protections are part of how Dukan ERP is built.

Read the privacy policySecurity pageSupport
  • Passwords and PINs are stored only as one-way hashes, never in readable form.
  • Changes to business records are written to an audit trail that the application itself cannot edit or delete.
  • Permissions are granted per person and per store, and the server checks them on every request.
  • Signed-in sessions can be ended, and devices removed from an account, at any time.
